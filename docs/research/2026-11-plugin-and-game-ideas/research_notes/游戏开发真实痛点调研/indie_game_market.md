# Indie Game Market 2023–2026: What Succeeds, and What a 1–3 Person AI-Assisted Team Can Realistically Build

Scope note: every number below comes from a third-party estimate unless it is labelled "developer-announced". Valve publishes no per-game sales data. Estimates come from review-count multipliers (Boxleiter-style), VG Insights/Sensor Tower, Gamalytic, SteamData and similar trackers, and they can be off by 2x or more. Research covered about 19 searches and fetches. Several titles named in the brief could not be verified (see Gaps).

## 1. Steam genre/tag performance 2023–2025: medians, hit rates, oversupply

### Takeaway
The median Steam game earns very little: about $4.8K gross across 107 sub-genres. Only about 2.4–3.0% of yearly releases reach 1,000 reviews. Hit *rates* are highest in Open World Survival Craft, Farming, Roguelike Deckbuilder, Simulation/Management, Horror and Idle. Puzzle and 2D platformers are badly oversupplied: about 4,000 puzzle releases in 2025 had a 0.34% hit rate.

### Cited Findings
**Hit rate (share of releases reaching 1,000 reviews; Chris Zukowski / How To Market A Game, VG Insights data sampled Jan 4, 2026)** — [HTMAG "What the hell happened in 2025?"](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/)
- Releases and hits per year: 2022 had 12,304 releases and 337 hits (2.74%). 2023 had 13,834 and 354 (2.56%). 2024 had 18,239 and 445 (2.44%). 2025 had 20,282 and 608 (2.99%). 579 of the 608 hits in 2025 were AA/indie games.
- Hit rates by genre, 2025 vs 2024 (hits/releases):

  - Farming: 5/60, 8.3% (20.83%)
  - Roguelike Deckbuilder: 11/212, 5.1% (6.71%)
  - Simulation: 43/1,048, 4.1% (3.76%)
  - Management: 19/549, 3.4% (5.43%)
  - Horror: 39/1,208, 3.2% (1.81%)
  - Idle: 27/965, 2.79% (3.05%)
  - RPG: 28/1,158, 2.4% (1.46%)
  - Racing: 14/654, 2.1%
  - Tower Defense: 9/511, 1.76% (1.44%)
  - Automation: 5/420, 1.19% (1.57%)
  - Metroidvania: 3/269, 1.1%

  - 3D Platformer: 8/1,390. The article lists this as "0.05%", but the arithmetic gives about 0.58%, so it is probably a typo.
- Top genres by number of hits in 2025: Narrative 51, Simulation 43, Horror 39, RPG 28, Idle/Incremental 27, Roguelike 22, Sexual content 21, Multiplayer Shooter 21, Shooter 21, Management 19. Sexual content made up 1,846 releases (9.1% of all releases).
- Method: "hit" means 1,000+ reviews, though the author notes some games reach $150K+ with about 500 reviews. Each game was assigned one genre by hand. IP games and Chinese-only games were excluded.

**Median gross revenue by Steam tag (Immutable, licensed sales-estimate dataset, pulled 2026-09-10; median revenue counts paid titles only; gross before Steam cut, VAT and refunds)** — [Immutable](https://www.immutable.com/insights/steam-genre-revenue-benchmarks)

**Revenue concentration (GameDiscoverCo, games with lifetime revenue over $1M)** — [Game World Observer summary](https://gameworldobserver.com/2025/07/01/analytics-three-quarters-of-game-revenue-on-steam-comes-from-action-and-rpgs)
- Share of revenue: Action 58.37%, RPG 17.11%, Strategy 13.97%, Simulation 9.76%. These are lifetime shares, not 2024 medians, and the figures come via the search snippet.
- A secondary analysis of 2024 AA/indie games with at least $500 revenue (Gamalytic/GameDiscoverCo data) found high medians for Simulation, Strategy, Roguelite/Roguelike and Anime/NSFW, and low medians for Puzzle, Arcade, Platformer and Top-Down. This comes from a search summary of a Reddit-based analysis, so it is low confidence — [Josh Hardy, 2024 data analysis](https://www.joshhardy.co.uk/post/indie-game-revenue-on-steam-key-insights-from-2024-data-analysis).
- GameDiscoverCo also ranks genre tags by median pre-release "hype" (followers and wishlists) — [GameDiscoverCo: genres with most hype 2024](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-pc-game-genres-have-the-most). The page was not fetched, so no figures are included here.

**Wishlist conversion**
- A median of about 10.5% of wishlists convert to first-week sales. This is a secondary source summarised in search results — [b3daily](https://b3daily.com/2025/10/25/how-indie-game-wishlists-on-steam-predict-success-trends-from-2024-2025).
- Secondary sources relaying Zukowski say crafty/buildy sim-strategy, horror and idle games convert wishlists well, while visual novels and 2D platformers convert poorly. I could not verify this in a primary post — [presskit.gg](https://presskit.gg/field-guides/how-many-wishlists-to-launch).

### Inferences
- Look at hit rate (hits/releases) together with median revenue. Horror and Idle each have a low median (about $2.6–2.9K) because they are flooded with cheap entries, but a decent hit rate (2.8–3.2%) and many absolute hits (39 and 27). That shape is "cheap to try, winner-takes-most". It suits a tiny team running many small bets.
- Puzzle and 2D platformer are the worst risk/reward combination: the most supply, the lowest medians and the lowest hit rates. Avoid them unless the game has a strong hook.
- Open World Survival Craft and Farming have the best ratios, but they are content- and 3D-heavy and usually out of scope for 1–3 people. Roguelike deckbuilders (5.1%), Management/Sim (3.4–4.1%) and Idle (2.8%) fit tiny teams better.
- The 2025 hit rate rose to 2.99% as total releases grew. More games broke out in absolute terms (608) despite about 20K releases.

### Gaps
- The VG Insights / Gamalytic genre medians themselves were not fetched. Immutable is the only primary median table.
- GameDiscoverCo's exact 2024 per-tag median revenue table is paywalled or was not found.
- Zukowski's "genres that sell" median-wishlist-by-genre table was not retrieved.

## 2. Breakout small-team hits 2023–2026: scope, team size, dev time, why they worked

### Takeaway
2023–2025 Steam mega-hits were overwhelmingly tiny-team, cheap (under $10–15) games. They fall into a few patterns: (a) "friendslop" online co-op with physics/voice comedy (Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., PEAK, Webfishing); (b) roguelite systems games that run on number-go-up (Balatro, Megabonk, Ball x Pit); (c) mundane-job or crime simulators (Supermarket Simulator, Schedule I); (d) tiny-scope tension games (Buckshot Roulette).

### Cited Findings
**R.E.P.O., PEAK and Schedule I together**
- Three 2025 indie games (R.E.P.O., PEAK and Schedule I) sold more than 42M copies and grossed $377.5M on Steam in 2025. These are third-party estimates (SteamData/VG Insights/Alinea) — [Medium: Steam in 2025](https://medium.com/@research_86150/steam-in-2025-the-year-indie-games-ate-the-industry-alive-666abf04551f); [VoxBooster indie stats 2026](https://voxbooster.com/blog/indie-game-statistics-2026/).

**PEAK** (Aggro Crab + Landfall)
- Built by 3 Aggro Crab and 4 Landfall staff. Most production happened during a **month-long retreat in Seoul (Feb 2025)**, with polish afterwards. It launched in June 2025.
- Budget was **under $200K** in total. The price was **under $5** with a 38% launch discount.
- Sales (developer-announced): 100K in 24 hours, 1M a few days later, 1.6M after one week, 2M+ later. Both studios broke even within hours.
- Quotes: "We avoided scope creep by having a built-in ship date: midsummer"; "the game jam IS the game"; "text is evil".
- Sources: [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/how-co-op-climbing-hit-peak-achieved-2-million-sales-for-less-than-200-000-); [Landfall press kit lists 7 developers](https://landfall.se/peak-press-kit).
- GamesRadar headline reports **11M copies** after "a few months of work" — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/co-op/after-a-few-months-of-work-led-to-11-million-copies-sold-on-steam-peak-devs-embrace-what-many-companies-refuse-to-learn-were-not-going-to-continually-have-a-graph-go-up/).
- Other sources give revenue of about $87M and up to 15.7M units. Estimates conflict, likely because of different dates — [VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/indie-game-statistics-2026/).

**R.E.P.O.** (semiwork)
- About 18.4M copies (2026 roundup), with Alinea estimating revenue around $147M — [VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/indie-game-statistics-2026/).
- Peak concurrent players: 271,571 (search snippet) — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/top-7-indie-games-highest-player-count-steam).
- Team size was not found.

**Schedule I** (TVGS, Tyler's Video Game Studio; solo developer)
- Over 2M copies early in Early Access (2025) — [DeepNewz](https://deepnewz.com/video-games/drug-selling-simulator-schedule-tops-steam-sales-over-2-million-copies-sold-on-201648d3).
- A review-based estimate gives **5.9M–12M copies and $118M–$236M gross** from about 295K reviews — [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/games/3164500).
- Another tracker cites $151M and 8M units — [VoxBooster](https://voxbooster.com/blog/indie-game-statistics-2026/).
- Wikipedia entry: [Schedule I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schedule_I_(video_game)).

**Lethal Company** (Zeekerss, solo; Early Access mid-Oct 2023)
- About 10M copies in its first months — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/lethal-company-sold-an-estimated-10-million-copies).
- Later estimates: 11.9M copies and $95M gross ([Raijin](https://raijin.gg/app/1966720/Lethal_Company)); 15.0M and $126.9M ([SteamData](https://steamdata.ai/game/1966720/lethal-company)); 15.1M and $119M ([Sensor Tower/VGI](https://app.sensortower.com/vgi/game/lethal-company)).

**Balatro** (LocalThunk, solo; publisher Playstack; released Feb 2024)
- 3.5M copies sold by Dec 2024 and 5M+ by Jan 2025 (developer/publisher-announced) — [Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/12/12/balatro-3-5-million-copies-sold-buckshot-roulette-4m); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balatro).

**Buckshot Roulette** (Mike Klubnika, Estonian solo developer)
- 4M units by Dec 10, 2024, and reportedly 8M+ by Dec 2025 — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/investment/item-46509); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckshot_Roulette).
- It had about 9K concurrent players and a 96% Steam rating in 2024 — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-43247).
- The scope is tiny: one table, a few items and a single loop, plus a later multiplayer mode.

**Megabonk**
- 1M copies in 2 weeks (developer-announced, Sept–Oct 2025) — [Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2570423/megabonk-reaches-one-million-sales-in-two-weeks-joining-indie-gamings-biggest-2025-successes).
- Described as solo-developed (with a separate composer), but the developer's identity is speculated and unconfirmed — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megabonk); [ResetEra: dev withdrew Game Awards nomination](https://www.resetera.com/threads/megabonk-developer-withdraws-game-awards-nomination.1357348/).

**Ball x Pit** (Kenny Sun with a small team; Devolver Digital)
- 100K units one day after launch, 300K in five days, and 1M+ by Dec 1, 2025 (about 6 weeks after launch) — [Outrun Gaming](https://outrungaming.com/indie-roguelite-ball-x-pit-sells-300000-copies-in-five-days/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ball_x_Pit).
- The design is a mash-up of a brick-breaker and a survivors-like/base builder.

**Webfishing** (lamedeveloper, 2024)
- Estimates: 1.3M copies and $6.0M ([SteamData](https://steamdata.ai/game/3146520/webfishing)); 1.7M units and $6.7M ([Sensor Tower/VGI](https://app.sensortower.com/vgi/game/webfishing)).

**Supermarket Simulator** (Nokta Games, 2024)
- Estimated $94.4M gross and $27.9M net to the developer — [Steam Revenue Calculator](https://steam-revenue-calculator.com/app/2670630/supermarket-simulator).
- Team size was not verified.

**CloverPit** (2025) is a slot-machine roguelite in the Balatro/Luck Be a Landlord lineage — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CloverPit). Sales were not retrieved.

### Inferences
- **Pattern A: friendslop co-op.** Examples are Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., PEAK, Webfishing, Content Warning and YAPYAP. Traits: low price ($5–10), stylised low-poly art, proximity voice chat, physics jank as comedy, and 4-player sessions that generate clips. These games sell in "4-packs" because friends buy together. Development is short: PEAK's core was built in about 1 month. However, they need networked 3D, which is a moderate engineering burden that AI coding agents can help with.
- **Pattern B: systems roguelites with "number goes up".** Examples are Balatro, Luck Be a Landlord, CloverPit, Ball x Pit, Megabonk, Brotato and Vampire Survivors. They are solo-buildable, lean on UI and 2D art, and are driven by balance and synergy. The work is mostly systems and tuning, which suits AI-assisted iteration well.
- **Pattern C: job/crime/business simulators.** Examples are Supermarket Simulator, Schedule I and the wave of shop simulators. They are first-person 3D with mundane loops and expansion and progression. Streamer-friendly, and solo development is possible.
- **Pattern D: micro-scope tension or horror.** Buckshot Roulette (one room) and Mouthwashing are examples. The tiny scope with high atmosphere suits short horror, which had a 3.2% hit rate in 2025.
- Common traits: price $3–$15; one clearly communicable hook that a single GIF or clip can explain; replayability from systems rather than handcrafted content; Early Access or fast iteration after launch.

### Gaps
- No verified numbers were retrieved for Vampire Survivors, Content Warning, Brotato, Dome Keeper, Peglin, Luck Be a Landlord, Shapez 2, Mouthwashing, Liar's Bar, Blue Prince or Bongo Cat. Searches returned nothing usable within the budget, so do not cite figures for them without checking.
- Team size for R.E.P.O. and Supermarket Simulator is unverified. Megabonk's developer identity is unconfirmed.
- Exact dev time is verified only for PEAK (about 1 month core, about 4 months to launch).

## 3. Web game hits/genres (Poki, CrazyGames, itch HTML5) and WeChat/Douyin mini-games

### Takeaway
Western web portals are ad-monetised, hypercasual-to-hybridcasual markets with exploding supply: 15K+ browser games were released in H1 2025 alone. Meme waves (Brainrot), merge, puzzle, .io/multiplayer and simulators drive plays. The Chinese mini-game market (about ¥53.5B in 2025) has shifted toward mid-core: tower defense, idle RPG, SLG and Legend. IAA (ad-monetised) charts are still dominated by casual puzzle games, which is the realistic entry point for tiny teams.

### Cited Findings
**Western web portals**
- WebGameAnalytics monitors 25K+ web games: 18K+ on Yandex, 4.5K+ on CrazyGames and 2.2K+ on Poki.
- 2025 new releases: about 300 on Poki and about 900 on CrazyGames. Yandex had about 23K published, with only about 7.6K still active at year-end; games rated 30 or below for 3+ weeks are delisted.
- Average rating of 2025 games: CrazyGames 89%, Poki 81%, Yandex 80%.
- More than 15K browser games were released in H1 2025, more than 2021–2023 combined (Playgama study cited).
- The **"Brainrot" meme wave hit all three platforms**: Steal Brainrot Online on CrazyGames had 900K+ votes; Merge Rot and Brainrot Craft on Poki; Steal Brainrot on Yandex.
- Yandex favourites: case simulators (#1 and #2 by votes), IP knock-offs, and text-messaging simulators. Expected for 2026: Roblox-like games on the web.
- Source: [WN Hub interview with WebGameAnalytics](https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-49660).
- Poki claims 60M MAU, 2.6x CrazyGames. 40% of Poki players are on mobile (Similarweb estimates 32%).
- Popular web genres: hybridcasual, match, merge and casino. Monetisation is mostly ads, and web ads monetise below mobile. IAP is growing but rare.
- Revenue shares vary: Arkadium gives developers 75%; Gameflare takes 50%.
- HTML5 game revenue was $1.03B (2021), projected to reach $3.09B by 2028 (Google/Kantar, 2023).
- Source: [Naavik: Web Gaming Strikes Back](https://naavik.co/digest/web-gaming-strikes-back/).
- An unofficial claim says Poki had 625M players, 11.1B gameplays and 227 new releases in 2025, with merge up 80% YoY and sort and screw/physics puzzles rising. This is low reliability, an unverified GitHub report — [GitHub html5-game-opportunity-report](https://github.com/pocketagent98-ai/html5-game-opportunity-report).
- CrazyGames is reputed for multiplayer and competitive casual games; Poki and Coolmath lean toward single-player and puzzle — [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/crazygames-vs-poki-vs-coolmath-games-2026/). This is a secondary source.

**WeChat / Douyin mini-games (小游戏)**
- 2025 domestic mini-program game revenue was about **¥53.535B** — [艾瑞 2026年小游戏行业热点专题报告](https://news.iresearch.cn/content/202607/560265.shtml) (via search summary).
- In the IAA model, **more than 80% of the top-100 games by revenue are 休闲益智 (casual puzzle)**. In the hybrid-monetisation top 100, 模拟策略 makes up about 70%, with tower defense ranking high. Over the past year the fastest-growing categories were SLG, 塔防, 二合 and MMO, and 放置 did not boom as it did in 2023–2024 — [17173 微信小游戏用户观察 2025](https://news.17173.com/content/06262025/192704046.shtml) (via search summary).
- WeChat mini-game bestseller (IAP) Top 100, from monthly GameLook/QQ reports:
  - Nov 2025: **14 tower defense games in the top 100 (8 in the top 50)**; headline says "head games getting heavier; 传奇, 塔防, SLG surging" — [QQ News Nov 2025](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20251202A08B4L00).
  - Jul 2025: RPG (incl. MMO) took about 58 of 100 slots, and casual games took more than 30% for several months — [QQ News Jul 2025](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250804A09H6Q00).
  - Oct 2025: tower defense and simulation surged, and 11 传奇 games were on the chart — [QQ News Oct 2025](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20251103A06D2U00).
  - Feb 2025: "SLG and idle still strong, tower defense winning big" — [GameLook Feb 2025](http://www.gamelook.com.cn/2025/03/565558/). The full text was not fetched because of a 503 error.
- Ad spend (DataEye):
  - War strategy was the largest WeChat mini-game ad-spend category in Jan 2025, at about 20%. By gameplay, 休闲, 益智 and 消除 are the top 3 — [界面新闻](https://www.jiemian.com/article/12333147.html).
  - Monthly advertised WeChat mini-games grew from 11,334 (Jan 2025) to 12,511 (Dec 2025). The IAP+hybrid share of advertisers fell from 27.06% to 22.00% — [DataEye 2025年度小游戏买量数据报告 PDF](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202601221818269698_1.pdf?1769097163000.pdf=).
  - Q3 2025: idle's share of ad spend was declining, simulation/management was strong, and the number of tower defense games being advertised rose more than 7% QoQ — [163/DataEye Q3](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KBOGT56A0553OKLX.html).
- Hit examples:
  - 《向僵尸开炮》 (tower-defense/roguelite shooter) stayed near the top of the chart.
  - 《永远的蔚蓝星球》 blends merge and roguelite.
  - 《合金战线》 combines the Metal Slug IP with roguelite tower defense.
  - Source: [163 2025 report](https://www.163.com/dy/article/JROUI2QE0553OKLX.html).
- Industry framing: "大厂围猎、小团队求生" (big studios hunting, small teams surviving). Mini-games became harder for small teams in 2025 as big companies moved in — [网易 2025](https://m.163.com/dy/article/KGT45CO4052685Q5.html).

### Inferences
- For a 1–3 person team, **WeChat/Douyin IAA casual puzzle** games (消除, sort, screw, 羊了个羊-style difficulty spikes, merge) remain the accessible lane. IAP/hybrid charts (tower defense roguelite, idle RPG, SLG) need ad-buying budgets (买量) that tiny teams lack.
- On Western portals, success is about fast, meme-responsive iteration. Brainrot, case-opening and merge variants appear and fade within months. Many cheap shots, quickly shipped, suit AI coding agents and generative art well.
- Web portals pay little per player through ads. A useful strategy is web as a prototype and funnel (Poki/CrazyGames/itch), then a Steam release. Several Steam hits (e.g. idle and desktop games) started as web or jam games, but this was not verified in this research.

### Gaps
- No official Poki or CrazyGames genre play-share data was found. Their developer blogs were not fetched.
- No confirmed 2025 data on Douyin mini-game genre rankings was found. The 羊了个羊 numbers for 2022 were outside scope and not retrieved.
- itch.io HTML5 top-genre data was not found.

## 4. Player demand signals: Next Fest, streamers, clips, co-op, short sessions

### Takeaway
Next Fest winners in 2025 were dominated by **co-op "friend slop"** and idle/desktop games, and almost every breakout had a **pre-festival spark**: a viral TikTok, a creator clip campaign, or a long-running demo. Clip-ability (Medal, TikTok) and friend-group purchases are the strongest demand engines for tiny teams.

### Cited Findings
**Steam Next Fest, October 2025** — [HTMAG Next Fest Oct 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/10/20/steam-next-fest-october-2025-checking-in-on-the-games-that-broke-through/)
- **YAPYAP** (online co-op horror, "friend slop"): 200K+ wishlists before Next Fest; #2 most-played demo; 50K clips on Medal in one week; TikTok announcement about 1.5M views.
- **LORT** (co-op action roguelite, 1–8 players; the team's first Steam game): 45K wishlists before the demo, then 68K, gaining about 17K during Next Fest for about 85K total. It used a 500-player playtest and the jestr.gg creator platform (2M+ views, 57 videos).
- **Desktop Defender** (idle/incremental desktop game; team has 9+ prior games): 200 followers before the fest, then **20K wishlists during it**, with 900 concurrent users.
- **Cairn** (The Game Bakers): 200K+ demo downloads and about 400 creators contacted.
- Zukowski defines "friend slop" as fun, quickly developed co-op games with easy mechanics.
- Wishlist tiers: under 1K (weak); 1–5K (solid, no spark); 20K–300K (games with a viral spark). About 7K wishlists is the threshold for Popular Upcoming.

**Next Fest benchmarks, March 2025** — [HTMAG benchmarks](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/03/26/benchmarks-how-many-wishlists-can-i-get-from-steam-next-fest/)
- The top earners with low demo conversion were mostly co-op friend-slop games. This comes from the search summary; the page was not fetched in full.

**Desktop/idle "companion" games**
- Desktop Defender and Bongo Cat (unverified numbers) show demand for always-on, sit-on-your-desktop idle toys. Idle had 27 hits in 2025 and a 2.79% hit rate ([HTMAG 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/)).

**Price and impulse buys**
- PEAK's sub-$5 price was explicitly cited as making it an "impulse spend" ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/how-co-op-climbing-hit-peak-achieved-2-million-sales-for-less-than-200-000-)).
- Schedule I's growth was attributed to going viral on streams ([DeepNewz](https://deepnewz.com/video-games/drug-selling-simulator-schedule-tops-steam-sales-over-2-million-copies-sold-on-201648d3)).

### Inferences
- Design for **the clip**: a 10–30 second moment of emergent failure, physics comedy, a jump scare or a huge number pop that someone wants to share. Proximity voice, ragdolls, betrayal (Liar's Bar, Buckshot Roulette) and synergy explosions (Balatro, Megabonk) all generate clips.
- Co-op sells in groups. One streamer playing with 3 friends advertises to 4 audiences.
- Ship the demo early and run a creator/clip campaign. Next Fest amplifies existing momentum; it does not create it.
- Short sessions (5–20 minute runs) match both streaming segments and web/mini-game play patterns.

### Gaps
- Median wishlists gained per Next Fest game, by genre, was not retrieved. Zukowski's 200+ game survey was referenced but not fetched.
- No quantitative TikTok or Twitch genre data was found.

## 5. Genres suited (and unsuited) to AI-assisted production

### Takeaway
Generative AI is already widespread on Steam: about 20% of 2025 releases disclosed it, mostly for visual assets. Disclosure is self-reported, so the market is flooded with AI-assisted games, and **AI use is not a differentiator**. AI agents give the most leverage for systems-driven, UI-heavy, procedurally varied 2D games, which overlap with the higher hit-rate tiny-team genres: roguelite deckbuilder, idle/incremental, management sim and survivors-like.

### Cited Findings
- **7,818 Steam titles (about 7% of the library) disclose generative AI use**, up from about 1.1% (about 1,000 titles) in Apr 2024. **Just under 20% of 2025 releases** disclose it. About 60% of disclosures involve visual asset generation.
- Disclosure is self-reported, so the figures are a lower bound. Steam's updated rules require disclosure only for player-facing AI content.
- Sources: [Totally Human Media](https://www.totallyhuman.io/blog/the-surprising-new-number-of-genai-games-on-steam); [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/pc-gaming/1-in-5-steam-games-released-in-2025-use-generative-ai-up-nearly-700-percent-year-on-year-7-818-titles-disclose-genai-asset-usage-7-percent-of-entire-steam-library); [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/software/ai/the-boffin-behind-valves-steam-labs-says-the-number-of-steam-releases-featuring-genai-in-2025-is-1-in-5-with-7-percent-of-all-games-on-there-now-incorporating-it-weve-octupled-last-years-figure/).
- PEAK's team said "text is evil", deliberately minimising narrative and localisation burden for global virality ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/how-co-op-climbing-hit-peak-achieved-2-million-sales-for-less-than-200-000-)).
- Narrative games had the most hits in 2025 (51), but Visual Novel, Adventure and Point-and-Click have low medians or hit rates (VN $10.3K median across 12,646 games; P&C 0.18% hit rate) ([Immutable](https://www.immutable.com/insights/steam-genre-revenue-benchmarks); [HTMAG](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/)).

### Inferences
These inferences are reasoned from the evidence above, not directly measured.
- **Well suited**, where systems, numbers, UI and procedural variety dominate and art can be stylised or iconographic:
  - roguelike deckbuilders and slot/poker roguelites (Balatro, CloverPit, Luck Be a Landlord lineage)
  - survivors-likes and hybrids (Megabonk, Ball x Pit)
  - idle/incremental and desktop-idle (Desktop Defender)
  - management/shop sims with modular assets
  - tower defense roguelites (which also lead the WeChat charts)
  - merge, sort and screw puzzles for web and mini-game platforms
  - social deduction or bluffing table games with a single scene (Buckshot Roulette, Liar's Bar)
  - low-poly co-op physics comedy, where networking code is the main cost and AI agents can help
- **Poorly suited**:
  - content-heavy narrative (writing quality is scrutinised, players are hostile to AI text, localisation costs, and it is oversupplied)
  - high-fidelity 3D or open-world survival craft, which has a high hit rate but is content-scale-bound
  - hand-crafted level design such as platformers and metroidvanias (oversupplied, low hit rate)
  - anything whose selling point is art quality
- **Risk**: with about 1 in 5 new releases using generative AI, AI-looking art in a capsule image may reduce click-through. This is not quantified here. A cohesive, deliberately stylised look (pixel, low-poly, UI-minimal) mitigates it.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data was found comparing sales or reviews of AI-disclosed versus non-disclosed games.
- No data was found on Steam player sentiment toward AI disclosure, or on how often negative reviews mention AI.
- No case studies of hits that publicly credit AI coding agents were verified.
